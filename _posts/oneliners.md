While the scientists in the video were trying to understand how smart the animals are, the animals were understanding them perfectly and reacting accordingly. On the contrary, those species do understand us pretty well, being equally unknown to human mode of communication. I think scientists should rather think on how smart we are? This self-analysis might help mankind understand nature more closely and survive longer.
